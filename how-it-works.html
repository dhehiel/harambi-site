Process

How it works

Crowd-building turns participation into durable African capacity. The cycle compounds: each successful project increases credibility, capability, and trust.

The crowd-building cycle

  1. 1 Public capital + capability + labour
  2. 2 Community participation
  3. 3 Verified building
  4. 4 African enterprise
  5. 5 Shared value and tiered benefits
  6. 6 Increased African productive capacity
  7. 7 More capital. More capability. More building.

Two contribution pathways

General contribution

Contribute to the Harambi General Treasury. Allocation follows the Harambi Allocation Index and governance. You join the system’s direction.

Directed contribution

Select a specific project or brand. Your contribution routes directly to that project’s treasury. You join a particular build.

Contribution is broader than money

Expertise, skilled labour, ideas, networks, technology, governance, mentorship, mobilisation, feedback, validation — all are recognized in the co-builder framework.

Trust spine

Participation rests on progressive trust: identity → permission → contribution → governance. Verification and accountability are structural, not optional.

The frontier is open to those ready to build inside the structure.
