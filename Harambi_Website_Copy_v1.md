# ABOUT `/about`

### ATTENTION
**What is Harambi**

Harambi is an African institutional architecture for increasing self-sustainability and sovereignty.  
It is not crowdfunding. It is crowd-building.

---

### INTEREST — The core idea
Traditional path: give money → receive a product or equity.  
Harambi path: contribute capital, capability, labour, knowledge, network, resource, or governance → participate in building → help increase Africa’s capacity to build and own what is built.

The community does not merely fund an enterprise.  
The community helps constitute the capacity and the ecosystem.

---

### INTEREST — Ubuntu as structure
The root is Ubuntu: *I am because we are.*  
In Harambi this is not sentiment. It is design.

Value is created relationally.  
The strength of the project is the strength of the community.  
No one — including the founder — stands outside the covenant.

---

### DESIRE — Mission
**African Sovereignty.**  
Increasing Africa’s productive, technological, economic, cultural, institutional, and strategic capacity so Africans can increasingly determine and build their own future.

Harambi’s contribution is specific:  
Building African enterprises through African participation, so that what Africa builds, Africa owns.

---

### DESIRE — Two streams
**Kora** — Commons operating system. Collective capacity. The Accord.  
**Coforge** — Enterprise formation system. Institutional capacity. The Compact.

One institutional trust layer. Two units of coordination.

# HOW IT WORKS `/how-it-works`

### ATTENTION
**How it works**

Crowd-building turns participation into durable African capacity.  
The cycle compounds: each successful project increases credibility, capability, and trust.

---

### INTEREST — The cycle
1. Public capital + capability + labour  
2. Community participation  
3. Verified building  
4. African enterprise  
5. Shared value and tiered benefits  
6. Increased African productive capacity  
7. More capital. More capability. More building.

---

### DESIRE — Two pathways
**General contribution**  
Contribute to the Harambi General Treasury. Allocation follows the Harambi Allocation Index and governance. You join the system’s direction.

**Directed contribution**  
Select a specific project or brand. Your contribution routes directly to that project’s treasury. You join a particular build.

---

### DESIRE — Contribution is broader than money
Expertise, skilled labour, ideas, networks, technology, governance, mentorship, mobilisation, feedback, validation — all are recognized in the co-builder framework.

---

### INTEREST — Trust spine
Participation rests on progressive trust:  
identity → permission → contribution → governance.  
Verification and accountability are structural, not optional.

# COFORGE `/coforge`

### ATTENTION
**Coforge**  
The enterprise formation system of Harambi.

Coforge originates large-scale, multi-stakeholder opportunities and convenes the companies and institutions required to co-create and hold them from the beginning — rather than building alone and seeking partners afterward.

---

### INTEREST — Purpose
Complex problems do not always need one organization.  
Sometimes the better enterprise is the system that enables those who already hold the required capabilities to build the solution together.

---

### DESIRE — The Compact
The coordination principle of enterprise formation.  
How institutions, capital, and capability holders align contribution, governance, and shared holding of what is built.

---

### DESIRE — Who it is for
Companies, institutions, capital holders, and capability holders ready to co-form ventures around real opportunities — not only to fund them after the fact.

---

### INTEREST — Place in Harambi
Coforge is the enterprise expression of Harambi.  
It shares the institutional trust layer with Kora, and coordinates institutions and multi-stakeholder formation.

# COVENANT `/covenant`

### ATTENTION
**The Harambi Covenant**

A covenant for participation in the Harambi Network.  
This page is a public summary. The downloadable document is a curated public version with restricted contents.

---

### INTEREST — Central proposition
Harambi’s knowledge may travel freely;  
Harambi’s institutional trust must be earned and governed.

---

### DESIRE — Principles (public summary)

**Purpose**  
Harambi exists so African people, communities, organizations, and builders can increasingly create, finance, coordinate, own, and sustain the infrastructure necessary for their collective future.

**Knowledge vs institutional trust**  
Inspiration is open. Participation in the Network and use of designated mechanisms require adherence to the Covenant.

**Integrity of identity**  
Unauthorized representation as a Harambi institution is prohibited. The name, network, and institutional authority are protected.

**African self-sustainability**  
Participation must align with increasing African capacity and ownership — not extractive use of collective infrastructure without reciprocal contribution.

**Accountability**  
Institutions and participants handling resources through Harambi infrastructure are accountable under the Covenant.
