Commons

Kora

The Commons operating system of Harambi. Kora organizes people, knowledge, resources, and capabilities around shared needs and opportunities — converting dispersed participation into structured, accountable capacity.

Purpose

Kora exists so collective participation becomes visible, useful, and capable of producing durable African capacity. It is not a romantic idea of community. It is an operating system for it.

The Accord

The coordination principle of the Commons. How participants align around shared needs, contribution, and accountability within Kora.

Who it is for

People, communities, and individual contributors ready to put capital, capability, labour, knowledge, or network into collective building.

Place in Harambi

Kora is the Commons expression of Harambi. It shares the institutional trust layer with Coforge, and coordinates the unit of people and communities.
