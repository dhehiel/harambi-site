Enterprise

Coforge

The enterprise formation system of Harambi. Coforge originates large-scale, multi-stakeholder opportunities and convenes the companies and institutions required to co-create and hold them from the beginning — rather than building alone and seeking partners afterward.

Purpose

Complex problems do not always need one organization. Sometimes the better enterprise is the system that enables those who already hold the required capabilities to build the solution together.

The Compact

The coordination principle of enterprise formation. How institutions, capital, and capability holders align contribution, governance, and shared holding of what is built.

Who it is for

Companies, institutions, capital holders, and capability holders ready to co-form ventures around real opportunities — not only to fund them after the fact.

Place in Harambi

Coforge is the enterprise expression of Harambi. It shares the institutional trust layer with Kora, and coordinates institutions and multi-stakeholder formation.
