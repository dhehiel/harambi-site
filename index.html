Crowdfunding

Crowd-building

Crowd-building asks the crowd to become the project.

Harambi exists so participation does not stop at support. It continues into structure, capacity, and ownership.

Two systems

One mission. Two expressions.

Kora

Commons operating system

Collective capacity. The Accord. For people, communities, and individual contributors.

Coforge

Enterprise formation system

Institutional capacity. The Compact. For companies, institutions, and capability holders.

Both serve the same destination: Building African capacity through African participation, so that what Africa builds, Africa owns.
