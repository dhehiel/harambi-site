About

What is Harambi

Harambi is an African institutional architecture for increasing self-sustainability and sovereignty. It is not crowdfunding. It is crowd-building.

The core idea

Traditional path: give money → receive a product or equity.

Harambi path: contribute capital, capability, labour, knowledge, network, resource, or governance → participate in building → help increase Africa’s capacity to build and own what is built.

The community does not merely fund an enterprise. The community helps constitute the capacity and the ecosystem.

Ubuntu as structure

The root is Ubuntu: I am because we are. In Harambi this is not sentiment. It is design.

Value is created relationally. The strength of the project is the strength of the community. No one — including the founder — stands outside the covenant.

Mission

African Sovereignty. Increasing Africa’s productive, technological, economic, cultural, institutional, and strategic capacity so Africans can increasingly determine and build their own future.

Harambi’s contribution is specific: Building African enterprises through African participation, so that what Africa builds, Africa owns.

Two streams

  • Kora — Commons operating system. Collective capacity. The Accord.
  • Coforge — Enterprise formation system. Institutional capacity. The Compact.

One institutional trust layer. Two units of coordination.

  • Not a typical crowdfunding website
  • Not an extractive platform that takes without reciprocal contribution
  • Not controlled outside the institutional covenant
