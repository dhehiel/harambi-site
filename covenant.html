The Harambi Covenant

A covenant for participation in the Harambi Network. This page is a public summary. The downloadable document is a curated public version with restricted contents.

Harambi’s knowledge may travel freely; Harambi’s institutional trust must be earned and governed.

Purpose

Harambi exists so African people, communities, organizations, and builders can increasingly create, finance, coordinate, own, and sustain the infrastructure necessary for their collective future.

Knowledge vs institutional trust

Inspiration is open. Participation in the Network and use of designated mechanisms require adherence to the Covenant.

Integrity of identity

Unauthorized representation as a Harambi institution is prohibited. The name, network, and institutional authority are protected.

African self-sustainability

Participation must align with increasing African capacity and ownership — not extractive use of collective infrastructure without reciprocal contribution.

Accountability

Institutions and participants handling resources through Harambi infrastructure are accountable under the Covenant.
